Your Learning Roadmap

A clear path from beginner to confident French speaker

A1

Beginner - Foundation

What you'll learn:

  • Greetings & introductions
  • Numbers, dates & time
  • Basic vocabulary (family, food, colours)
  • Simple sentence structure
  • Pronunciation basics
  • Reading & writing simple words
Duration: 3-4 months
A2

Elementary - Building Confidence

What you'll learn:

  • Everyday conversations
  • Past, present & future tenses
  • Describing people & places
  • Reading short texts
  • Writing simple paragraphs
  • Asking & answering questions
Duration: 4-5 months
B1

Intermediate - Independent User

What you'll learn:

  • Complex grammar structures
  • Expressing opinions & ideas
  • Reading & understanding articles
  • Writing structured essays
  • Participating in debates
  • Understanding native speakers
Duration: 5-6 months
B2

Upper Intermediate - Fluent Communicator

What you'll learn:

  • Advanced vocabulary
  • Nuanced expression
  • Professional writing skills
  • Complex listening comprehension
  • French for work & study
  • Cultural understanding
Duration: 6-7 months
C1

Advanced - Mastery

What you'll achieve:

  • Native-like fluency
  • Academic & professional French
  • Complex text analysis
  • Persuasive writing
  • Confident public speaking
  • Full cultural immersion
Milestone: DELF C1 Certified

About Frenchora

Learn French. Keep French. Live French.

Frenchora is an online French school and learning community created to help learners develop strong French communication skills in a structured, friendly and culturally connected environment.

The school was created with a special focus on francophone children living in English-speaking countries. Many of these children can understand or speak French at home, but they often struggle to read, write, spell and use formal French correctly.

Frenchora also welcomes beginners, non-francophone learners, teens and adults who want to learn French for school, family, work, travel, culture or personal growth.

Our aim is to make French learning clear, accessible, practical and enjoyable.

Our Programmes

Frenchora offers structured online French programmes for different types of learners.

Each programme is designed to support the learner's age, background, level and goals.

Learners are placed according to age, background and French level. We understand that learners do not all start from the same point which is why Frenchora offers an efficient placement system.

South African Consumer Protection Act (CPA) Compliance

Frenchora adheres to the South African Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008. All lesson cancellations require 24-hour notice. In the event of loadshedding or technical difficulties, makeup classes will be scheduled at no additional cost.

Refund Policy: If you are not satisfied after the first two lessons, we offer a full money-back guarantee. No questions asked.

POPIA Compliance - Privacy Policy

Frenchora is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). We collect, store, and process your data only for the purpose of providing our educational services.

Your rights: You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time. For data requests, please contact us at privacy@frenchora.co.za

Loadshedding & Technical Issue Policy

If a learner misses a lesson due to loadshedding or technical difficulties on either side, we will reschedule the lesson at no additional cost. We understand that connectivity issues can happen and we are committed to ensuring uninterrupted learning.
